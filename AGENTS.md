# Project instructions

Before changing this project, read [the project maintainer Skill](skills/classical-text-reader/SKILL.md) in full.

The Skill is mandatory for content, data, UI, API, migration, import/export, and persistence work. In particular, do not alter a saved note, its sentence ID, its original-text binding, its status, or its timestamp without explicit user authorization and a verified migration.

For any text added to the library, follow the editorial policy in [docs/EDITORIAL_POLICY.md](docs/EDITORIAL_POLICY.md). Do not present an unverified modern text as a uniquely authoritative original.
